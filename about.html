About.

A little on where I study and what kind of problems I like.

I'm a Cybersecurity major at PHINMA University of Iloilo, College of Information Technology Education. Most of my work has come out of capstone projects — built with the same care as production software, because the businesses using them are real.

I like systems with sharp edges: receipt numbering that has to match a government format exactly, discount rules that have to match the law, hardware that has to talk to Python over USB. That same instinct shows up in how I build — audit ledgers that can't be tampered with, two-factor logins instead of a CAPTCHA, role-based access baked in from the start. Security isn't a layer added at the end; it's part of the spec.
